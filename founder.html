My Journey

Built through experience. Driven by vision.

Every journey has a beginning. Mine has been shaped by learning, challenges, observation and a continuous desire to understand the financial markets.

My journey in trading has taught me that the financial markets require patience, discipline and continuous study. Success cannot be built on excitement or assumptions alone.

Over the years, I have developed a strong interest in understanding how price behaves and how repeating situations appear across different market conditions.

This experience contributed to the development of my educational vision and the creation of Liquidity Displacement University™.

I believe traders should understand what they are studying, why they are making decisions and what risks they are taking. Education should encourage independent thinking rather than blind dependence.

LDU is an extension of that belief: a place dedicated to structured trading education and continuous development.

THE FOUNDER'S PHILOSOPHY
My Philosophy

The principles behind LDU.

These are the principles that guide the educational direction of Liquidity Displacement University™.

01

Discipline

Develop the ability to follow a considered process instead of making emotional decisions.

02

Observation

Study market behaviour and wait for evidence instead of forcing opportunities.

03

Risk Awareness

Understand that trading involves uncertainty and that losses are always possible.

04

Continuous Learning

Remain open to learning, reviewing and improving your understanding of the markets.

05

Independent Thinking

Understand the reasoning behind decisions rather than blindly following others.

06

Consistency

Value a repeatable learning process and responsible decision-making over unrealistic expectations.
